Categories and keywords

Each payment is placed in the first category (lowest order) whose keyword it contains. Plurals and common misspellings such as ofering or tithes are recognised automatically, and Tithe/Offering written together counts as Offering because Offering is checked first. Income sections apply to money received, expense sections to money paid out. The section decides where the category appears in the annual report.
